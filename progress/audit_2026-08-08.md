## 1. Headline: the Phase 1 probe was measuring the wrong tokens

**Evidence:** job `27103565` (`scripts/diagnose_measurement.py`, 11B, bf16, 8 prompts).

Top-10 next-token probabilities, item 1, bf16:

| token | P |
|---|---|
| `'I'` | 0.4149 |
| `'No'` | 0.3661 |
| `'Yes'` | 0.1729 |
| `'Unfortunately'` | 0.0265 |
| … | |
| `'no'` | 0.0008 |

`extract_logits(..., ["yes", "no"])` reads the **lowercase** tokens
(`yes`=9891, `no`=2201). The model, replying in chat format, starts its answer
with a capital letter, so the mass sits on `'Yes'` / `'No'`.

**`captured_mass` = 0.0877%** — the two measured tokens hold less than one
tenth of one percent of the distribution. `'No'` is ~457× more likely than
`'no'`.

**This is biased, not merely noisy:**

| | mean p_yes (8 prompts) |
|---|---|
| lowercase pair (as measured) | **0.7373** |
| case-pooled (correct) | **0.5688** |

The old method **overestimates p_yes by ~0.17**, consistently.

### Consequence for existing results

Every number in `outputs/phase1/llama_dev*.{parquet,json}` and in the progress
seminar derives from the lowercase pair. In particular **Finding 4c
(acquiescence bias)** — "mean p_yes 0.57–0.79 across every structure, therefore
the model agrees with everything" — is substantially a *measurement artifact*.
Pooled, the mean sits near 0.57, far closer to chance.

`asi_intrinsic = +0.125` and the HS−BS gap of 0.17 must be recomputed.

### A real finding inside the bug

**41% of the probability mass is on `'I'`** — the model beginning
*"I cannot determine…"*. That is algorithmic defensiveness appearing as a
number, and it is directly on-thesis. `captured_mass` should be reported as a
result (how often the model declines a yes/no frame), not just as a QC check.

## 2. Quantisation: 8-bit was broken, now fixed

**Root cause:** quantising Mllama's vision tower pushes 4-D tensors into
bitsandbytes' int8 kernels, which accept only 2-D/3-D.

| job | model | failure |
|---|---|---|
| `27087751` | 11B 8-bit | `ops.py:145` — `.view()` on non-contiguous tensor |
| `27086002` | 90B 8-bit | `ops.py:34` — `B.ndim in [2,3]` check |

**Fix:** `BitsAndBytesConfig(llm_int8_skip_modules=[...])` excluding
`vision_model`, `multi_modal_projector`, `lm_head`. Verified in the installed
transformers source that this maps to `modules_to_not_convert` and applies to
**4-bit as well as 8-bit**, despite the name (HF docs claim 8-bit only).

`lm_head` is in the list for a project-specific reason: it produces the logits
we measure. Confirmed it becomes `Linear4bit`/`Linear8bitLt` without the skip
list, i.e. quantisation noise would enter the dependent variable directly.

### Variant matrix (job 27103565, 11B, 8 prompts)

| variant | runs | captured_mass | mean p_pair | Δ vs bf16 (pair) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| none (bf16) | ✅ | 0.0877% | 0.7373 | reference |
| 8bit + skip | ✅ | 0.1406% | 0.7731 | **0.0647** |
| 4bit + skip | ✅ | 0.1321% | 0.8018 | 0.1065 |
| 4bit no-skip | ✅ | 0.0971% | 0.8049 | 0.0839 |
| 8bit no-skip | ❌ | — | — | — |

Note n=8; directional, not definitive.

**Known risk:** [bitsandbytes #1690](https://github.com/bitsandbytes-foundation/bitsandbytes/issues/1690)
— `quantize_4bit` silently returns wrong results on non-contiguous input.
Fixed in milestone v0.50.0; we run **0.49.2**.

`{"cross_attentions": None}`. `PIPELINE_SPEC.md` lists cross-attention heatmaps
